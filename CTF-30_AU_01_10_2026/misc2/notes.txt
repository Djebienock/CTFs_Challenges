notes - side project (the "where do i actually keep going" thing)

ok the idea, before i forget it again.

my phone already logs step count, and it can see every wifi access point
around me with a signal reading. problem: i keep ending up somewhere on
autopilot and i honestly couldn't tell you where i go the most. so --
cross-reference the two things i already have.

the insight (i think this is right): a place that actually MATTERS shows
up as me physically moving around near the same access point, again and
again. not just a strong signal. my router at home is the loudest signal
by a mile and that is obviously not some mystery "place" worth finding.
loud just means close. close doesn't mean i went anywhere.

so the whole trick is: only count a reading when the step counter says i
was actually on the move around that time. if i was sitting still, throw
it out, doesn't matter how strong the signal was. stationary = noise.
figuring out where "sitting still" ends and "moving" begins took me
forever, i kept nudging it.

and not every access point should get equal credit. the ones i actually
recognize and trust should carry more than some random hotspot i walked
past once on the street. i listed the ones that count in aps.conf with a
little number beside each -- bigger number = i trust it more. the numbers
are just relative, don't read too much into them.

then per access point it's basically: how strong, leaning on how much i
trust the spot, but only from the times i was moving. tally it up. the one
that rises to the top is the answer.
